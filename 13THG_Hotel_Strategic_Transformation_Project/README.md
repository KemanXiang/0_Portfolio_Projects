# Business Model Adaptation and Strategic Transformation

### An Integrated Qualitative and Quantitative Case Study of THG Hotel

**Research portfolio | Hospitality strategy · Management interview analysis · Investment evaluation · Strategic decision-making**

## Project Overview

This project examines how a **hospitality organization** can respond to rising operating costs, limited booking growth, and constraints on room-price increases through differentiated services, customer-oriented offerings, external partnerships, and operational innovation. It combines management interview analysis with customer and operational assessment, five-year investment evaluation, and an integrated strategic feasibility assessment.

The central contribution is a decision framework that links **financial attractiveness** to **organizational readiness, partner dependencies, and measurable incremental value**. Rather than treating a positive net present value (NPV) as sufficient grounds for investment, the analysis evaluates what each strategy would require to succeed in practice.

**Companion report:** `THG_Hotel_PhD_Portfolio_Research_Report.pdf` .

## Research Questions

**Main question:** How can a hospitality business facing cost pressures and constrained pricing power adapt its business model through service innovation, operational improvements, and external partnerships?

The study addresses three related questions:

1. What strategic and operational constraints shape the hotel's choices?
2. How might differentiated services, digital capabilities, and external partnerships create and capture value?
3. How should management combine investment evaluation and qualitative implementation evidence when prioritizing strategic initiatives?

## Research Design and Methods

The project uses an integrated single-case study design, combining qualitative analysis and quantitative investment evaluation.

|Component|Approach|Research output|
|---|---|---|
|Management Interview Analysis|Coding of 20 management question–response pairs|58 meaning units, 10 codes, 5 themes|
|Operational and Customer Analysis|Assessment of cost drivers, customer segments, demand and service opportunities|Business constraints and segment-specific opportunities|
|Investment Evaluation|Five-year discounted cash-flow analysis across seven initiatives|Five-year NPV comparison|
|Integrated Strategic Feasibility Assessment|Synthesis of financial outcomes, coded interview evidence, and implementation requirements|Strategy-level assessment and staged decision framework|

### Qualitative analysis

The management interview material covers strategic context, financial operations, customer segmentation, partnership ecosystems, technology and organizational capabilities, and implementation decisions. Responses were segmented into **58 meaning units**, coded using a **10-code codebook**, and synthesized into five cross-cutting themes.

|Theme|Analytical focus|
|---|---|
|Margin discipline and value capture|Cost pressure, pricing constraints, incremental contribution|
|Segment-sensitive service design|Customer heterogeneity, personalization, loyalty value|
|Ecosystem-based service extension|External partners, coordination, service accountability|
|Implementation capacity and digital change|Staff, facilities, systems integration and operational readiness|
|Evidence-based staged decision-making|Pilots, measurement, decision thresholds and scaling|

### Investment evaluation

The financial assessment compares seven strategic initiatives using five-year incremental cash-flow models. **Five-year NPV** refers to the discounted value calculated by the investment model; it is not a measure of profit already realized by THG Hotel. The model results depend on the inputs and assumptions documented in the analytical workbooks.

|2|Premium-service upselling|$2,342,990|Verify uptake, inventory capacity and contribution margin|
|3|Corporate events|$1,335,915|Assess facility, staffing and sales capabilities|
|4|Local partnership bundles|$1,283,232|Define partner responsibilities and net economics|
|5|Digital check-in and mobile key|$1,203,503|Test system compatibility and realized labor savings|

|7|Seasonal themed rooms|$655,379|Test willingness to pay and renovation-related costs|

**Interpretation:** All seven initiatives show positive five-year NPV in the report's base-case model. Their relative values do not establish actual realized returns or, by themselves, determine rollout priority. The qualitative findings highlight conditions that should be checked before committing resources.

## Integrated Strategic Feasibility Assessment

The study links each initiative's financial result to management interview themes and practical execution requirements. The proposed decision architecture has four gates:

|Gate|Decision task|Evidence needed|
|---|---|---|
|1. Baseline validation|Establish the current operational and economic position|Occupancy, segment behavior, unit margins, property capabilities|
|2. Limited feasibility testing|Evaluate targeted pilots or technical and facility feasibility|Take-up, partner fulfillment, systems compatibility, staff readiness|
|3. Incremental value confirmation|Test whether additional value remains after relevant costs|Incremental contribution, cannibalization, exceptions, service quality|
|4. Scale decision|Expand, revise or discontinue an initiative|Predefined financial and operational thresholds, accountable owner|

The stage-gated approach is a **research-derived management recommendation**, not a statement that THG Hotel has already executed these stages.

## Business Relevance

The project is relevant to hospitality firms—and more broadly to service businesses—facing constrained pricing power, uncertain demand response, cross-organizational dependencies, and implementation trade-offs. It demonstrates research skills in interview coding, evidence synthesis, investment appraisal, and translating analysis into actionable management recommendations.

## How to Review the Project

1. **Start with the report:** Read the Abstract, Research Methodology, Management Interview Analysis, and Integrated Strategic Feasibility Assessment.
2. **Inspect the qualitative evidence:** Use the meaning-unit coding CSV and final codebook to trace findings to interview responses.
3. **Review investment calculations:** Open the financial analysis workbook to examine cash-flow inputs, formulas and five-year NPV results.
4. **Check the integrated assessment:** Compare the seven initiatives' financial values with the coded implementation conditions and decision recommendations.

## Limitations

This is a single-case analysis. The management Q&A does not, by itself, establish that all discussed measures were implemented. The investment evaluation uses modeled cash flows and requires validation against authorized operating and transaction-level records before its outputs can be interpreted as realized business impact. The case materials and the analytical framework support strategic propositions, not causal claims about intervention effects.
